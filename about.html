Since 2024

From a few chickens to a working farm

The Walker family started Walker’s Edge Farm in 2024 with a simple goal: build a sustainable, family-run farm that provides high-quality food to our local community.

What began as a small homestead with a few chickens has grown into a diverse operation. We now raise Highland cattle, myotonic goats, and Kunekune pigs, and we produce eggs, vegetables, and pumpkins.

We believe in ethical animal husbandry, sustainable practices, and connecting people with where their food comes from. Our animals are raised with care, free to behave naturally, and help keep our land healthy.

Animal welfare first

Pasture, space, and daily care for every animal, with guardian dogs on watch around the clock.

Sustainable practices

Grazing animals keep pastures healthy, and our vegetables are grown without synthetic pesticides.

Family operated

When you pick up eggs, you’re buying from the family that raised the hens.
