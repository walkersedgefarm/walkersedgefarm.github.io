Our story

A small homestead that grew into a working family farm.

The Walker family started Walker’s Edge Farm in 2024 with a few chickens. Today we raise Highland cattle, myotonic goats, Kunekune pigs, ducks, and laying hens, along with vegetables and pumpkins.
